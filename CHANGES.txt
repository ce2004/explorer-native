1.0.2
The update window lists every change on its own line. Tab to the list and read it with the arrow keys, then Tab to Update or Cancel.
Release notes are plain text, with no symbols for the screen reader to read out.

1.0.1
Google Drive takes your client ID and client secret in two fields in Preferences, instead of a JSON file.
Pressing OK checks the ID and secret with Google. If Google refuses them, Preferences stays open on the field to fix.
When Google accepts them, they are saved encrypted, Drive turns on and the browser opens to sign in.

1.0.0
First release on GitHub, for x64 and ARM64 PCs.
Help menu with Check for updates and About.
Updates install over the running copy and restart it on the new version.
No Google credentials ship with the app.
Everything saved in AppData is encrypted for your Windows account: settings, the Google client, the sign-in and the logs.
